## Welcome to the LinkedIn Autopilot Beta!
Thank you for participating in our controlled beta. Your feedback helps ensure our LinkedIn publishing and AI Studio platform meets the highest standards of reliability.

### Step 1: Sign Up & Verify Email
1. Visit https://autopilot.gtinnox.in/register
2. Register with your work email and password.
3. Check your inbox for the confirmation link and click to verify.

### Step 2: Connect Your LinkedIn Profile
1. Navigate to **LinkedIn Accounts** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Connect Personal Profile**.
3. Authorize via official LinkedIn OAuth 2.0.
4. Verify your profile name and avatar appear with an **Active** badge.

### Step 3: Configure Your Brand Voice
1. Go to **Settings** > **Brand Settings**.
2. Set your industry, tone, target audience, and preferred hashtags.
3. Click **Save Brand Settings**.

### Step 4: Generate Content in AI Studio
1. Navigate to **AI Studio** in the sidebar.
2. Select **7-Day Plan**, enter your core topic, and click **Build 7-Day Calendar**.
3. Review the 7 generated posts, tweak hooks or hashtags, and click **Schedule All 7 Posts**.

### Step 5: Report Feedback or Issues
- If you notice anything unexpected, click the **Help / Support (? / LifeBuoy)** icon in the top right navigation to submit a ticket directly to GT InnoX engineering.
